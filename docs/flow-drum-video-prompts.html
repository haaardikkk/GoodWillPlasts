Google Flow: open mouth drum manufacturing video

Six 8-second clips that show a blue HDPE open mouth drum being made, ending on the finished drum with the GoodWill Plast logo. Stitch them in order to get one ~48-second film. Clip 6 also works on its own as a short looping hero video.

Settings for every clip

  • Mode: Text to Video (clips 1–5), Frames to Video (clip 6, see below)
  • Aspect ratio: 16:9, length: 8 seconds, best-quality model
  • To keep clips flowing into each other: after a clip is done, use Extend (or take its last frame as the start frame of the next clip) and paste the next prompt.

Step 0: make the logo drum image first (ChatGPT, 1 minute)

Video models often garble logos. So make one still image with the logo correct, then animate it in clip 6.

In ChatGPT, attach src/assets/logo(1).png (your logo) and src/assets/Products/open_top/open_mouth_drum.png (the drum), then paste:

Avoid list (add to the end of any prompt that comes out wrong)

Text
Avoid: warped or melting objects, extra limbs, distorted hands, garbled text or logos,
changing drum shape, flickering, shaky camera, cartoon look, watermarks.
